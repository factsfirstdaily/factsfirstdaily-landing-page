North Carolina Recount Seals a Historic House Win for Democrats

Democrat Chuck Hubbard unseated Republican Rep. Virginia Foxx in North Carolina's 5th District on Sunday after a recount. The BBC reports Hubbard won by 330 votes with 99% counted. Foxx had held the seat since 2005.

The result gives Democrats at least 258 House seats, NBC News projects. NBC reports, citing the House historian, that no party has held a larger House majority this century. Four races had yet to be called as of Sunday.

Democrats have gained 44 seats so far, four more than their 2018 midterm gain, the Guardian reports. Two Louisiana races go to a December runoff. Democrats also won 56 of the 100 Senate seats.

Why it matters. The result sets up divided government for the final two years of President Trump's term. Incoming Speaker Hakeem Jeffries told CBS he will focus on affordability, health care and immigration, the Guardian reports. He also backed banning members of Congress from trading stock.

Army Moves Iowa Blast to Wednesday as a Court Fight Continues

The U.S. Army plans to detonate several tons of unstable explosives at an Iowa munitions plant on Wednesday. The Army announced the new date in a social media post Sunday night, CBS News reports. A day earlier, it had told a court it would act by Tuesday.

A federal appeals court had paused the detonation indefinitely after environmental groups sued. The BBC reports the Army intends to proceed under an emergency exemption. The material is degraded lead azide, a volatile compound used as a detonator, stored at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant in Middletown.

Government lawyers said the chemicals could explode on their own once temperatures drop below 30 degrees. Earthjustice attorney Thien Chau said the Army seemed focused on sidestepping the judicial process, AP reports via the Guardian.

Why it matters. Iowa Senator-elect Josh Turek called for a full investigation into how the stockpile was allowed to degrade, CBS News reports. Locals fear a dust plume could carry toxic lead particles into Iowa and Illinois communities. The Army says a controlled detonation is far safer.

Libya Seeks an Inquiry After Ukraine Claims It Sank a Russian Warship

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Ukrainian sea drones sank the Merkury, a Russian corvette, at the Benghazi naval base in eastern Libya on Sunday. He said seven Ukrainian-made drones carried out what he called an exemplary operation, CBS News reports.

Eastern Libya's government asked the UN secretary-general and Security Council for an international investigation, the AP reports via NPR. It wants to know whether western Libya was used to launch the attack. Libyan officials told the AP at least 10 people were wounded.

Zelensky said a second vessel, a cargo ship he claimed carried weapons, was also hit, the BBC reports. The UN mission in Libya expressed serious concern and urged an independent investigation, CBS News reports. It warned that Libya must not become a battleground for foreign conflicts.

Why it matters. Ukraine's SBU security service called both vessels legitimate military targets, NBC News reports. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said Moscow had noted Zelensky's statement and deferred to the defense ministry. Libya has been split between rival governments since 2011.

Trump Hosts Meme Coin Dinner as Democrats Eye Crypto Probes

President Trump hosted a dinner for top holders of his meme coin on Sunday at his golf club in Sterling, Virginia. It was his third such cryptocurrency gala, the Guardian reports. Guests stood in the rain to enter.

The $TRUMP coin peaked at $74 but now trades below $1, NBC News reports. NBC reports Trump's net worth has risen by nearly $3 billion since he returned to office in 2025. More than $1 billion came from family-linked cryptocurrencies.

Trump told attendees he backs the coin for U.S. competitiveness, saying China would do it otherwise, the Guardian reports. The listed winning wallets held $8.6 million, down from $148 million for a May 2025 dinner.

Why it matters. Democratic senators wrote that the dinner appeared built to let the president enrich himself, NBC News reports. Democrats take control of Congress in the new year. They have signaled his crypto ventures will face oversight.

Why the Collapse of the COP31 Climate Talks Matters

What happened

UN climate talks in Antalya, Turkey, broke up on Sunday without their central political agreement. The BBC reports the last comparable collapse came in Copenhagen in 2009. The final draft drew objections because it left out any direct reference to fossil fuels. Poorer countries also said it lacked clear finance pledges from richer nations.

Why it matters

The BBC reports the draft had acknowledged that the world is expected to pass 1.5C of warming. Without a deal, that recognition has been lost. The text would also have mentioned carbon dioxide removal, which no final COP agreement has explicitly done before.

What each side says

The United States sent no representatives for a second year in a row, CBS News reports. UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres said there is no ambiguity in the science. More than 90 countries backed a separate pledge to raise electricity's share of energy use to 35% by 2035, CNBC reports.

What comes next

UN climate chief Simon Stiell told the BBC he would seek advice on reforming the process. The next summit, COP32, is set for Ethiopia in November 2027. Former U.S. Vice President Al Gore said the COP process needs significant reform. Conference rules require unanimous agreement for any deal. Questions are growing over whether consensus talks can still deliver.
