01 / The Headlines

The main stories.

Oil Crashed 7% in a Single Day — Here’s Why

Crude fell more than 7% and settled under $100 a barrel after President Trump said Iran wants to make a deal. Traders pulled back from bets that the conflict would widen after Tuesday's midterms. Attacks on tankers and shipping lanes could still keep prices higher than they were before the war.

Source: CNBC

Apple’s New CEO Just Aced His First Earnings Report

Revenue rose 12% to $114.5 billion in Apple's fiscal fourth quarter, a record for the period and ahead of what Wall Street expected. It was a welcome result after a rough earnings season for big tech, where several of Apple's peers disappointed investors.

Source: Apple Newsroom, ABC News

A $40-a-Year HIV Prevention Shot Just Got Approved

Regulators approved a generic version of lenacapavir, the only HIV prevention shot given just twice a year. It's expected to cost about $40 per person per year in 120 low- and middle-income countries, a fraction of the brand-name price.

Source: France 24, CBC News

Microsoft Just Hit $81 Billion in Revenue — and Azure Crossed $100 Billion a Year

Microsoft beat Wall Street expectations with $81.3 billion in quarterly revenue, up 19%. Azure cloud revenue crossed $100 billion on an annualized basis for the first time, powered by surging demand for AI workloads. CEO Satya Nadella called it the company's strongest AI quarter yet.

Source: Star Tribune, HotHardware

Savings Bonds Now Pay 5.51% — the Best Rate Savers Have Seen in Years

New Series I savings bonds are paying 5.51% a year through April 2027. Rising inflation lifted the rate, making I-bonds one of the safest high-yield options for Americans who want to park cash without market risk. The fixed-rate component also rose, locking in a higher floor for the life of the bond.

Source: TreasuryDirect

02 / The Context

The background.

Millions of Teenagers Are About to Own SpaceX Stock

What happened

SpaceX President Gwynne Shotwell and her husband are giving one share of their SpaceX stock to each of more than 2 million children, deposited into their Trump Accounts starting in December. The gift is aimed largely at teens in lower-income areas.

How we got here

Trump Accounts are child savings accounts created by the 2025 tax law. Babies born from 2025 through 2028 can get a $1,000 deposit from the Treasury, and families and donors can add more.

Why it matters

SpaceX is one of the world's most valuable private companies, and its shares are normally out of reach for ordinary investors. For many of these kids, it will be the first stock they've ever owned.

What to watch

The shares are being distributed through the nonprofit Invest America program beginning in December. Parents should watch for eligibility details and how the shares appear in their child's account.

Source: Gwynne Shotwell on X, Benzinga
