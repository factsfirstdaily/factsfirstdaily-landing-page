Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information Facts First Daily collects when you visit factsfirstdaily.com or subscribe to our newsletter, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you subscribe.
  • Signup details recorded by our email provider, such as the date and time you subscribed and your IP address. We use these to confirm your subscription and prevent abuse.
  • Email engagement, such as whether an email was opened and which links were clicked. This helps us understand what readers find useful.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To send you the Facts First Daily newsletter.
  • To send occasional messages about the newsletter itself, such as confirming your subscription.
  • To improve the newsletter.

We do not sell or rent your email address.

Who processes your data

We use MailerLite to store our subscriber list and send emails. MailerLite processes your information on our behalf and may store it on servers outside your country. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you and may keep a record of your email address so we don't contact you again by mistake.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of every email.
  • Access, correct, or delete your information by emailing us.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
